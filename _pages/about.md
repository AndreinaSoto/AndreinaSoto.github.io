I am a historian and anthropologist with professional experience in research and program management. As a **researcher**, I examine the ways people of African descent contested and reshaped institutions, norms, and imperial geographies during the early modern period. As a **program manager**, I have designed, implemented, and evaluated **digital humanities** and **public history** programs, workshops, and events. 

Previously, I served as Associate Director of the [**Caribbean Digital Scholarship Collective**](http://cdscollective.org/), an initiative funded by the Mellon Foundation that supports the growth and development of digital scholarship on the Caribbean and its diaspora. I am also part of the team behind [**Neogranadina**](https://neogranadina.org/), a nonprofit organization that uses novel digital tools to digitize, process, and promote Latin America's historical heritage.
