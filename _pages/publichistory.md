I have worked with different organizations to develop programming, articles, educational materials, and digital resources that highlight the importance of historical research and archival holdings for specialized and non-specialized users. 

## [The Albert Lepage Center for History in the Public Interest](https://www1.villanova.edu/villanova/artsci/lepage.html)

As Center Administrator of the Lepage Center, I researched, planned, and executed funded programs, workshops, and public events that reflect the Center’s mission of empowering the public with historical tools to examine contemporary issues. 

## [The Public Historian](https://online.ucpress.edu/tph)

As Editorial Assistant for this important publication, I copy-edited upcoming articles, flagged errors based on journal citation guidelines, and managed the UCSB Public History Program social media.

## [Santa Barbara Mission Archive-Library](https://www.sbmal.org/)

As an archival intern for the Mission, I helped sort and organize archival collections about Santa Barbara's colonial past and supported fundraising events. 

## [Historical Society of Pennsylvania](https://www.portal.hsp.org/)

As an education intern, I created lesson plans on Philadelphia's local history using the HSP's archival holdings. These lesson plans include [The Jewish History of Philadelphia, 2016](https://www.portal.hsp.org/unit-plan-items/unit-plan-10)and [Immigration and Cultural Diversity in East Harlem, 2016](https://www.portal.hsp.org/unit-plan-items/unit-plan-13).
