I have worked with different organizations developing programming, articles, educational materials, and digital resources aimed at bringing attention to the importance of historical research and archival holdings for specialized and non-specialized users. 

## [The Albert Lepage Center for History in the Public Interest](https://www1.villanova.edu/villanova/artsci/lepage.html)

In my role as Center Administrator of the Lepage Center, I researched, planned and executed different funded programs, workshops, and public events that reflect the Center’s mission of empowering the public with historical tools to examine contemporary issues. 

## [The Public Historian](https://online.ucpress.edu/tph)

As the Editorial Assistant of this important publication, I copy-edited upcoming articles and flag errors based on journal citation guidelines, and managed the UCSB Public History Program social media.

## [Santa Barbara Mission Archive-Library](https://www.sbmal.org/)

During my time working as Archival intern for the Mission, I helped sort and organize archival collections about Santa Barbara's colonial past, and supported fund-raising events. 

## [Historical Society of Pennsylvania](https://www.portal.hsp.org/)

As an education interns, I created lesson plans about Philadelphia's local history using the archival holdings of the HSP. These lesson plans include [The Jewish History of Philadelphia, 2016](https://www.portal.hsp.org/unit-plan-items/unit-plan-10)and [Immigration and Cultural Diversity in East Harlem, 2016](https://www.portal.hsp.org/unit-plan-items/unit-plan-13).
